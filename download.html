How to install

HALA MOVE is on Google Play and the App Store. Two taps install it straight from your phone's store, and updates arrive on their own.

STEP 1

Tap your store button above

Pick Driver if you have a bakkie, van or truck and want to earn, or Customer if you need something moved. Then tap Google Play on Android or App Store on iPhone. It opens the app straight in your store.

STEP 2

Tap Install (or Get)

Your store handles the rest. If you installed an earlier APK of HALA MOVE on Android, uninstall that one first.

STEP 3

Sign up & go

Open HALA MOVE, enter your phone number, verify the OTP, and you're in. Drivers complete the quick verification wizard.
