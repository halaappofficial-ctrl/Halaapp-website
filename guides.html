How to use HALA MOVE

Each guide follows the real screens, one step at a time: booking a delivery on the web, paying cash at a store's till, running a business account, delivering from an online store, and watching a fleet.

Names, addresses and amounts on these screens are examples.

  • Book a delivery on the webFor anyone sending goods, from any phone or computer.
  • Pay cash at a store's tillFor shoppers at a store with our sign, and its cashier.
  • Run a business accountFor companies that book on account and pay one invoice a week.
  • Deliver from your online storeFor company accounts that sell on a WooCommerce website.
  • Watch your fleetFor fleet owners whose drivers deliver on HALA MOVE.
