HALA MOVE — how we talk about it

For the team. Not secret, but not indexed — please don't post the link publicly. Issued 2026-08-03. The facts live at https://www.halamove.co.za/brand-manifest.json; this page is the voice. If the two ever disagree, the facts file wins.

One rule above all the rest: never invent a number. Not drivers, not downloads, not ratings, not earnings. If you need a figure and it isn't in the facts file, ask Eric that day or leave it out. Every claim we have had to retract was a number nobody checked.

The one-sentence version

HALA MOVE is South Africa's real-time logistics marketplace: you post a move, verified drivers send you offers, you pick one, you track the vehicle live, and the money is held in escrow until the delivery is confirmed.

The wedge — say this early

A bakkie, a panel van or a truck cannot drive for Uber or Bolt — those move people. HALA MOVE is the only way that vehicle earns on demand.

This lands because it is not a comparison, it is a category. It also side-steps price arguments entirely, which is where we do not want the conversation.

Talking to a driver

Open with the vehicle, not the app

"Is that your bakkie?" beats any pitch. The vehicle is the asset; we are the demand.

The three things that actually close

  • You set your own price. Every job, you choose what you'll do it for. It is not a fixed rate handed to you.
  • Pioneer terms. The first 500 drivers pay zero commission on their first 20 completed jobs — they keep 100%. After that, keep up to 80%.
  • Same-day payout for jobs completed before 5pm. Cash out to your bank whenever you like (minimum R50, 2–3 business days to land).

Talking to a customer

  • Post a move in 30 seconds. You get a suggested fair price so you're not guessing.
  • Verified drivers send you offers — you choose, on price, rating and arrival time.
  • Your money sits in escrow (PayFast) and is only released once you confirm the delivery.
  • Track the vehicle live, and every item is photographed at loading and at delivery.

Objections, and what actually answers them

"How do I know the driver won't take my things?"

Every driver is identity-verified before their first job: SA ID checked against the government database, a face match against the Home Affairs photo for that ID, driving licence, vehicle registration, roadworthy and bank account — and a person reviews every driver before approval. Every item is photographed before loading and at delivery, timestamped. There is a one-tap SOS on every active job, for both sides.

Do not say "insured" or "covered". We have no goods-in-transit product yet. Say what is true and it is already stronger.

"Is it cheaper than the guy I use now?"

Don't answer on price — you'll be wrong and it isn't our claim. "You'll see real offers from verified drivers and you pick the one you want. You're not guessing what it should cost, and you're not paying until it lands." Never say "low commission" or "cheaper than Uber / Bolt / the movers".

"How many drivers do you have?"

"200+ drivers committed before we launched." That is the only driver figure that is approved, and note the word — committed, not "on board", not "ready and waiting". If they want today's live number, that comes from Eric on the day or not at all.

"What does it cost me as a driver?"

"Nothing to join, and no subscription. You keep up to 80% of each completed job — and if you're in the first 500 drivers, you keep 100% on your first 20 jobs." Frame it as commission, never a "platform fee": nobody pays us a fee, commission is simply what varies.

"Is it on iPhone?"

Yes — both apps are on the App Store as well as Google Play. Send them halamove.co.za/get (customer) or halamove.co.za/drive (driver) and the link sends their phone to the right store by itself. Never send a raw store URL: it's a dead end for whichever phone it wasn't meant for.

Words we use, and words we don't

  • "Offers", never "bids". Drivers send offers; customers compare offers. "Bid" stays in code and internal docs.
  • "HALA MOVE" in full, always. Never "HALA" on its own — the styled two-colour wordmark is a design treatment, not permission to shorten it.
  • "Suggested price" / "smart pricing", never "AI" or "algorithm".
  • "Identity-verified", never "background-checked". We check identity and documents; we do not run criminal record checks.
  • Bakkies, panel vans and trucks. If you say "bakkie" as the capability, say the full range in the same breath.
