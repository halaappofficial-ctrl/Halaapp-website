How furniture transport works on HALA MOVE

HALA MOVE is South Africa's real-time marketplace for furniture removal. You post your move once, verified drivers in Durban make offers on it live, and you pick the offer you want. The driver arrives and you track the move on a live GPS map from pickup to drop-off.

Built for the way people move now. Posting a job is free and commits you to nothing. You pay only when you accept an offer, and PayFast holds your money in escrow until the furniture is delivered.

1. Describe your load

Enter your pickup and drop-off addresses. Add a short description of what you’re moving: a couch, a bed, a full flat, or a specific list of items. Photos help drivers quote accurately, and Smart Quote shows a fair price range based on your load, distance and vehicle type.

2. Drivers offer in real time

Verified drivers in Durban see your job instantly. Each driver submits an offer with their name, star rating, vehicle photo and ETA. Offers stream in live, so you can pick based on price, rating, vehicle size or how quickly they can arrive.

3. Accept the offer you like

Choose the offer that suits you best. Your payment is charged and held in PayFast escrow. The driver is dispatched immediately and you get their live location.

4. Live GPS from pickup to drop-off

Watch your driver on a map. Know the exact ETA. Chat through the in-app chat if you need to give the driver a gate code or adjust the pickup point. Open the route in Google Maps or Waze if you want turn-by-turn directions yourself.

5. Confirm delivery and the payment is released

At drop-off, the driver takes a proof-of-delivery photo. You confirm receipt in the app. Escrow releases the payment, less HALA MOVE's 20% commission. Rate the driver and you’re done.

Delivery day checklist

A smooth furniture removal goes to plan when you prepare. Before your HALA MOVE driver arrives:

  1. Disassemble what you can. Beds, tables and bookshelves pack tighter when broken down. Keep fasteners in a labelled bag.
  2. Wrap fragile items. Mirrors, glass tabletops and electronics survive the trip when you wrap them in blankets or bubble wrap.
  3. Defrost the fridge/freezer. A wet fridge in the back of a bakkie ends badly. Unplug at least 12 hours before.
  4. Photograph your items before loading. It gives you your own record, so if a dispute arises you have a before-shot.
  5. Confirm parking on both ends. A driver who can’t park within 20 m of your door loses time you’re paying for.
  6. Have water and a tip ready. HALA MOVE drivers are independent contractors and tips are appreciated for heavy or complicated moves.
  7. Keep valuables with you. IDs, laptops, medication and jewellery travel with you, in your own bag.
